# Home and Notes direction: heuristic decision framework

Scores use a five-point ordinal scale: 1 means the direction conflicts with the stated portfolio goal; 3 means it partially supports the goal; 5 means it strongly supports the goal. These scores are a design-team assessment, not observed user behavior.

| Criterion | Current Home | Wholesale swap | Selective swap | Assessment basis |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Strongest work visible pre-scroll | 1 | 4 | 5 | Current Home shows destinations rather than project proof. Both swap options can surface Streaming Helper. |
| Information efficiency | 2 | 4 | 5 | The current Clarity Stack repeats destinations in Selected Work. A selective swap gives the hero and index distinct jobs. |
| Visual distinctiveness | 3 | 5 | 5 | The Notes composition is the site's most authored first viewport. Both swap options bring that visual strength to Home. |
| Content-role fit | 3 | 2 | 5 | A wholesale exchange risks making Home editorial and Notes project-led. The selective version preserves route intent. |
| Interaction clarity and access | 4 | 3 | 4 | Current destinations are understandable, but the hero's meaning depends partly on interaction. The selective version keeps proof legible at rest and treats motion as enhancement. |

The unweighted averages are 2.6 for the current direction, 3.6 for a wholesale swap, and 4.8 for the selective swap. The average is included only as a compact comparison of the documented rubric.
