# Education

programme Indiana University calls HCI/d, from Indiana University Bloomington.

He also has a computer science background. That is the phrasing to use: the site
does not state a computer science degree, so do not describe it as one.

The HCI/d degree is the reason the work spans research and implementation rather
than one or the other. The graduate capstone that became Streaming Helper, and
the INFO-I 543 Interaction Design Methods assignment behind the Porsche note,
both come from that programme.
