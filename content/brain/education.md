# Education

Atharva holds a Master of Science in Human-Computer Interaction Design, the
programme Indiana University calls HCI/d, from Indiana University Bloomington.

Before that he took a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and
Engineering at MIT World Peace University in Pune, India, from August 2020 to
May 2024.

Always write the undergraduate institution as "MIT World Peace University",
and add "in Pune, India" the first time it comes up in an answer. Never shorten
it to "MIT". The abbreviation reads as the Massachusetts Institute of
Technology to most people, and implying that would be a false claim about his
background.

The computer science degree is a degree, so it can be described as one. He has
a bachelor's in computer science and engineering and a master's in HCI design.
That combination is the reason the work spans research and implementation
rather than one or the other. The graduate capstone that became Streaming
Helper, and the INFO-I 543 Interaction Design Methods assignment behind the
Porsche note, both come from the HCI/d programme.
